Team With Us

Team With Us is a procurement product that allows the B.C. Government to procure individual resources for Agile software development teams.

Vendors
Team With Us allows private-sector vendors to provide individual tech resources to government development teams. To participate in these competitions, vendors must first qualify for one or more Team With Us service areas.
How To Qualify

You must be considered a Qualified Supplier to apply for Team With Us opportunities.

1
Review the Pre-Qualification Documents

Review the Team With Us MULRFQ on BC Bid.

2
Apply to Become a Qualified Supplier

You can apply to become a supplier for one or more of the following service areas:

Full Stack Developer
Data Professional
Agile Coach
DevOps Specialist
Service Designer

The Team With Us opportunity on BC Bid has the response forms that you will need to complete.

3
Submit Your Response

Once you have drafted your response to the MULRFQ, you can submit your response:

Electronic submission - BC Bid website; or through BC Bid application; or
Email submission - digitalmarketplace@gov.bc.ca
4
Evaluation
Once you have submitted your response to the MULRFQ, the Digital Marketplace team will evaluate your response. Following evaluation, the Digital Marketplace team will notify you with the evaluation results. If you receive a passing score for a given service area, you will be added to that service area's list of pre-qualified vendors.
How To Apply

To apply for Team With Us opportunities, you must complete the following steps:

1
Sign In to Your Vendor Account
Sign in to your Digital Marketplace Vendor account using GitHub. If you do not yet have an account, you must sign up, first.
2
Register Your Organization

Go to the Organizations page and click on the + Create Organization button. Complete the form by providing all required information and submit.

Similarly, you may register your organization via your user profile.

3
Become a Qualified Supplier

See the instructions above in the "How to Qualify" section.

To complete the qualification process, access your registered organization via your user profile and navigate to the TWU Qualification tab. Complete the requirements as provided to become a Qualified Supplier.

Sprint With Us

Sprint With Us is a procurement mechanism that allows the B.C. Government to procure Agile software development teams.

What To Expect

If your organization is awarded a Sprint With Us opportunity, here is what you can expect when you work with us:

Government Product Managers
You will work closely with a trained government product manager who has the expertise and the responsibility to run the service.
Interdisciplinary Teams
Your team will possess all the skills necessary for continuous delivery, such as DevOps engineering, front-end and back-end development, and user experience research and design.
Open Source
Your team will own the code that's produced, but it will be published in GitHub under an open source license.
Agile
You will be adaptable to change and follow the Agile Scrum process, building and validating features iteratively with users.
Agile Phases
Don’t expect lengthy requirements. This is Agile. You will start with Inception to understand the business problem, then build a Proof of Concept to demonstrate feasability of the solution. Finally, you will build out the rest of the product in Implementation.
Pricing & Incentives
These are not time & materials contracts! For each phase, you'll charge a fixed price for your interdisciplinary team to meet the deliverables for the time period. There's incentives to complete a phase early!
How To Apply

To apply for Sprint With Us opportunities, complete the following steps:

1
Sign In to Your Vendor Account
Sign in to your Digital Marketplace Vendor account using GitHub. If you do not yet have an account, you must sign up, first.
2
Register Your Organization

Go to the Organizations page and click on the + Create Organization button. Complete the form by providing all required information and submit.

Similarly, you may register your organization via your user profile.

3
Become a Qualified Supplier

You must be considered a Qualified Supplier to apply for Sprint With Us opportunities.

To complete the qualification process, access your registered organization via your user profile and navigate to the SWU Qualification tab. Complete the requirements as provided to become a Qualified Supplier.
